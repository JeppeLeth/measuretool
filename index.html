📐 How to use

  • Draw a red SCALE line over a known distance to calibrate.
  • Draw other lines over objects - the system will calculate lengths.
  • Double-click any label to edit its value.
  • Select an element and press DEL if you want to remove it.
    • - - - - - - - - - -
  • Use Area mode to draw a closed shape and calculate surface area.
  • Use Angle mode on two connected lines to measure the angle between them.
  • Save or load a project as JSON to continue your measurements later.
    • - - - - - - - - - -
  • Desktop: Left click to draw. Space + Drag (or Right Click) to pan.
  • Mobile: One finger to draw. Two fingers to pan and zoom simultaneously.
